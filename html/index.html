Four things people ask first

01What happens in a service? About 90 minutes of worship and a sermon. Come early, an usher will meet you and help you find a seat. 02When & where do you meet? Two campuses — Margaret Drive and Jurong — across multiple languages. 03Can I bring my kids? Yes. COOSkidz runs alongside weekend services with secure check-in and a programme for their age. 04What if I want to go deeper? Say hello over light refreshments at our Welcome Corner. We would like to know you!
