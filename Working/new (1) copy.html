Your first visit

You won't be singled out

We know walking into a church for the first time can feel daunting. So here's the honest version of what to expect — no surprises.

Read what to expect
ArriveCome ten minutes early. A host will meet you at the door and help you find a seat.
The serviceAbout 90 minutes. Songs, prayer, a talk. Stand or sit — whatever's comfortable.
Your childrenCOOSkidz runs alongside, with secure check-in and a programme for their age.
AfterwardsSay hello over coffee, or slip out quietly. Both are completely fine.
